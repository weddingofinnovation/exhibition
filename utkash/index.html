How does instant stall confirmation work? +

Our floorplans sync live with each organiser. Pick a position, e-sign in-platform, pay a 10% refundable deposit to hold — or pay in full for immediate allocation. Confirmation e-mail + calendar invite arrive in under 30 seconds.

Can I cancel or change my stall? +

Most shows offer free cancellation up to 30 days before doors open. Stall swaps are free within the same hall subject to availability. Cancellation rules are shown on every booking before payment.

What payment methods do you accept? +

UPI, credit/debit cards, NEFT/RTGS, and corporate wire for stalls above ₹10L. All transactions pass through PCI-DSS compliant gateways with TLS 1.3 encryption.

Do I have to use your design studio? +

No. Directory, booking and contract tools work on their own. Design & fabrication are opt-in services, billed separately — but available if you want a single invoice.

What does a custom stall cost? +

From ₹ 2,400/sqm for a modular build, up to ₹ 6,200/sqm for fully custom fabrication — all-inclusive of engineering, logistics, setup, dismantle. Line-item quote within 48 hours of brief.
