FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's included in the stall price?+

Shell scheme (for 9 sqm): carpet, fascia with company name, 3 spotlights, 1 plug-point, 1 table, 2 chairs. Custom-build stalls priced separately.

Can I change my stall size after booking?+

Yes, subject to availability. Upgrade within 14 days of booking at pro-rated price. Downgrade only up to 45 days before show.

How do I get my exhibitor badges?+

Digital badges issued 14 days before. Collect physical badges at organiser help desk using booking ID.

Is the show open to general public?+

Days 1–3 are trade-only. Day 4 is open to public with a ₹ 250 gate ticket. Trade visitors enter free with verified badge across all four days.

What's the dress code and official languages?+

Business smart on the floor. Awards night is black-tie. Official languages: English and Hindi. Translation in Japanese, German and Korean available at the international pavilion desks.

How do I bring product samples through customs?+

Use the official freight forwarder (BLR Logistics) for ATA Carnet handling and on-site clearance. Cut-off for sea-freight is 6 weeks before show; air-freight 10 days. Detailed customs guide is in the exhibitor manual sent post-booking.
